# Mechanics and settlement

How options are acted on and resolved. Definitions only.

**Exercise** The act, by the holder, of using the right the option grants, buying or selling the underlying at the strike.

**Assignment** The matching event for a writer, who is required to fulfill the obligation when a holder exercises.

**American-style** An option that can be exercised at any time up to expiration.

**European-style** An option that can be exercised only at expiration.

**Physical settlement** Resolution by actual delivery of the underlying asset.

**Cash settlement** Resolution by a cash payment equal to the in-the-money amount, with no delivery of the underlying.

**Expiration outcome** At expiration, an in-the-money option settles for its intrinsic value through exercise or cash, and an out-of-the-money option expires worthless.

**Early assignment** For American-style options, a writer can be assigned before expiration. This is a real possibility to understand, not a remote edge case.

**Liquidity** How easily a contract can be traded near its quoted price. Thinly traded options can be hard to exit, which is part of their risk, not a side note.
