# Full glossary

Alphabetical list of every term defined in this collection. Definitions only, no advice.

**American-style** Exercisable any time up to expiration.
**Assignment** A writer being required to fulfill the obligation when a holder exercises.
**At the money** Strike at or near the underlying price.
**Call** Right to buy the underlying at the strike.
**Cash settlement** Resolution by cash payment of the in-the-money amount.
**Contract size** Amount of underlying one contract represents.
**Delta** Sensitivity of option value to a one-unit move in the underlying.
**European-style** Exercisable only at expiration.
**Exercise** A holder using the right the option grants.
**Expiration** The date after which the option no longer exists.
**Extrinsic value** Premium beyond intrinsic value; decays with time.
**Gamma** Sensitivity of delta to a move in the underlying.
**Holder** The buyer who holds the right; long the option.
**Implied volatility** Expected movement implied by the option price.
**In the money** Strike favorable relative to the underlying; has intrinsic value.
**Intrinsic value** The amount by which an option is in the money.
**Liquidity** How easily a contract trades near its quoted price.
**Moneyness** Strike relative to the underlying price.
**Option** A contract granting a right without obligation to the holder.
**Out of the money** Strike unfavorable relative to the underlying; no intrinsic value.
**Physical settlement** Resolution by delivery of the underlying.
**Premium** Price paid by the buyer to the seller.
**Put** Right to sell the underlying at the strike.
**Rho** Sensitivity of option value to interest rates.
**Strike price** The fixed price at which an option can be exercised.
**Theta** Sensitivity of option value to the passage of time.
**Underlying** The asset the option is based on.
**Vega** Sensitivity of option value to implied volatility.
**Writer** The seller who holds the obligation; short the option.
