# The basics

The core building blocks of options vocabulary. Definitions only.

**Option** A contract giving the holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset at a set price within a set time. The seller of the contract takes on the matching obligation.

**Call** An option whose holder has the right to buy the underlying at the strike price.

**Put** An option whose holder has the right to sell the underlying at the strike price.

**Underlying** The asset the option is based on, such as a stock or an index.

**Strike price** The fixed price at which the option can be exercised.

**Expiration** The date after which the option no longer exists.

**Premium** The price paid by the buyer to the seller for the contract.

**Holder** The party who bought the option and holds the right. Also called long the option.

**Writer** The party who sold the option and holds the obligation. Also called short the option.

**Contract size** The amount of underlying one contract represents. For many listed equity options this is one hundred shares per contract, though this is a convention of those markets, not a universal rule.

**Open and close** Opening a position establishes it. Closing it ends it before expiration by taking the opposite trade.
